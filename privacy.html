Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This app is a personal, non-commercial tool used by its owner to connect their own Google Calendar account to their own Home Assistant installation.

Data collected and used

With the owner's consent, the app accesses Google Calendar data only: it reads events from, and writes events to, the calendars the owner chooses. This data is used solely to display those calendars in Home Assistant and to let Home Assistant automations react to events (for example, a work shift starting or ending).

Data sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Calendar data is not sent to any service other than the owner's own Home Assistant installation. No analytics or tracking is used.

Data retention

The app itself stores no calendar data. Events remain in the owner's Google Calendar account and in their Home Assistant installation, under the owner's control. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from their Google account settings, which immediately stops all access.
