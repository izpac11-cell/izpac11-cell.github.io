Home Assistant — Personal Calendar App

This is a small personal app used by its owner to connect their own Google Calendar account to their own Home Assistant installation, so household calendars (work schedules, appointments, and shared family events) can be shown in Home Assistant and used by Home Assistant automations.

It is not a public service, it is not offered to other users, and it does not collect, sell, or share personal information with anyone else.

How it works: the owner signs in with Google once, grants calendar access to this app, and Home Assistant reads and writes events on the calendars the owner chooses. No other data is requested.
